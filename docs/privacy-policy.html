Privacy Policy — Ghostly

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Ghostly does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no network code of any kind. It works entirely offline.

What the app stores

Ghostly saves a handful of settings in Android's private per-app storage on your device: whether the ghost is currently floating, his last position on screen, his size, whether taps pass through him, and whether vibration is enabled. This never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall Ghostly.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — draws the ghost on top of other apps. This is the app's entire purpose.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — Android requires an ongoing notification while the overlay runs; it carries the Stop button.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) — keeps the overlay alive while you use other apps.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — restores the ghost after a reboot, only if he was floating before.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — the optional buzz when the ghost bolts.

Ghostly requests no permission to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or any other personal information, and cannot read the contents of the screen or of any app it floats over.

Children

Ghostly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
