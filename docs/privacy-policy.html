Privacy Policy — Ghostly

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Ghostly works without an account and collects nothing unless you sign in. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and we never sell or share data with anyone.

Without an account

Your ghost lives entirely on your phone. Settings and his stats are kept in Android's private per-app storage, unreadable by other apps and deleted when you uninstall. The app makes one kind of network request: it downloads updated behaviour content (his reactions and voice) from our server. That request carries no account, no identifier, and nothing about you.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in lets your ghost follow you to a new phone and keeps his token balance honest. We then store, on our server:

  • Account: the stable Google account id, your email address and display name, as provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Your pets: species, name, appearance, personality settings, and their stats (hunger, energy, happiness, anger, sleeping).
  • Interaction history: when you fed, petted, played with, spooked or napped him. This is what lets him "remember" you.
  • Device: a per-app device identifier and the phone model, so a second device can restore the same pet.

We do not collect your location, contacts, files, the contents of your screen, which apps you use, or what you type. The app cannot see any of those.

Deleting your data

Open Ghostly → Settings → Delete my account and server data. This immediately and permanently removes your account, your pets, their stats and history from our server. The ghost on your phone stays. You can also request deletion by email — see how to delete your account.

Retention and security

Data is kept until you delete your account. It is stored on a server we operate, transmitted only over HTTPS, and is never used for advertising or profiling.

Children

Ghostly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
