Delete your Ghostly account

Deleting your account permanently removes everything Ghostly stored on its server for you: your account, your pets, their stats, their interaction history, your token balance and streak, and the record of which devices you used. It cannot be undone. The ghost on your phone is not affected.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Ghostly
  2. Go to the Settings tab
  3. Under Account, tap Delete my account and server data
  4. Confirm

By email

If you no longer have the app, email cvs.devs01@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, subject "Delete my Ghostly account". We delete the data within 7 days and confirm by reply.
